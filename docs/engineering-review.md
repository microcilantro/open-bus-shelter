# Engineering review list (Draft Rev A)

These items need a licensed engineer's analysis before any installation. Each has a matching GitHub issue label (`structural`, `anchorage`, `clearance`, `durability`). Numbers below are rough order-of-magnitude estimates for discussion only, not design values.

## 1. Post base moment (anchorage)

The two-post cantilever puts all roof wind and gravity load into two bases as overturning moment.

- Rough estimate: a 3048 x 1067 mm (10 x 3.5 ft) roof at about 1.0 to 1.4 kPa (20 to 30 psf) net uplift gives about 3.1 to 4.4 kN (700 to 1,000 lb) of force, or about 1.1 kN·m (10,000 in-lb) of moment per post.
- The concept base uses two 4-hole angle fittings per post and four screw anchors, with about 229 mm / 9 in anchor spread. Estimated anchor tension is roughly 3.1 to 3.6 kN (700 to 800 lb) per back anchor.
- **To check:** anchor capacity, fitting bending and prying, and whether a back-to-back post has enough bending capacity.

## 2. Anchor edge distance

With the post centred 152 mm / 6 in from the back of walk, the rear anchors sit about 38 mm / 1.5 in from the slab edge or joint.

- **To check:** edge-distance reductions. The post may need to move forward while keeping the base inside the 305 mm / 12 in post zone.

## 3. Slab thickness and embedment

Many sidewalks are only about 100 mm / 4 in thick, which limits anchor embedment.

- **To check:** minimum slab thickness, whether longer anchors are possible, and criteria for when a site needs a thicker pad.

## 4. Knee brace end connections

There is no 45° strut fitting at typical retail stores. The manual shows the connection as "per engineer's detail."

- **To check:** a connection made from stocked parts, or whether the braces can be omitted once the arm-to-post joint is checked.

## 5. Arm-to-post and beam-to-arm joints

Both joints use 90° angle fittings with spring nuts in the channel's open face.

- **To check:** spring-nut slip and pull-out values under wind uplift, and whether more bolts are needed.

## 6. Purlin spacing and roof panel span

Panels bear on purlins at 0, 914, 1524, 2134 and 3048 mm, so the largest span is 914 mm / 3 ft at the ends.

- **To check:** the panel maker's span tables for local wind and snow. Adding purlins over the arms brings the maximum span to 610 mm / 2 ft.

## 7. Bus mirror clearance (clearance)

On a 5 ft sidewalk the 4 ft deep roof edge ends 305 mm / 12 in from the curb face, at 2591 to 2845 mm / 8 ft 6 in to 9 ft 4 in high.

- **To check:** the local fleet's mirror height and reach, and whether to allow a shallower roof (about 762 mm / 2.5 ft) where it's tight.

## 8. Corrosion of retail galvanized steel (durability)

Electro-galvanized retail strut has a thinner zinc coat than hot-dip galvanized.

- **To check:** expected life by climate (especially coastal), the touch-up and inspection regime, and whether hot-dip galvanized should be required in some regions.

## 9. Code and accessibility compliance

- **To check:** accessibility rules for transit stops, local right-of-way encroachment permits, and the wind and seismic design basis for each jurisdiction.
