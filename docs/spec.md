# OBS-10 design specification (Draft Rev A)

## Goals

1. Every component available off the shelf at ordinary hardware or home-improvement stores, described generically so it can be bought anywhere.
2. No welding and no cutting of metal. Strut is used only in stock lengths (3048, 1219 and 610 mm / 10, 4 and 2 ft).
3. Two people with hand tools and one hammer drill can assemble and install it, ideally in one day.
4. Low cost, value-engineered at each step. Target under US$1,500 for the shelter and under US$3,000 complete.
5. Adoptable by transit agencies anywhere, with a local engineer's stamp.

## Configuration

- **Primary product:** two-post cantilever shelter, 3048 mm / 10 ft long. A wider shelter is out of scope.
- **Seating:** the bench is an optional add-on to the shelter or a stand-alone item. On sidewalks narrower than about 2134 mm / 7 ft, a leaning rail replaces the bench.
- **No rear wind panel.**

## Site geometry (5 ft sidewalk case)

| Zone | Depth from back of walk |
|---|---|
| Post zone (posts, base fittings, anchors) | 0 to 305 mm / 0 to 12 in |
| Clear pedestrian path | 305 to 1524 mm / 12 to 60 in (1219 mm / 4 ft minimum) |
| Roof | 0 to 1219 mm / 0 to 48 in, adjustable 914 to 1219 mm / 36 to 48 in by panel overlap |
| Roof edge to curb face | 305 mm / 12 in on a 5 ft walk (flagged: confirm bus mirror clearance) |

- Posts are 2438 mm / 8 ft apart on centres, 152 mm / 6 in from the back of walk.
- The roof slopes about 1:12 along its length. Clear height is about 2591 mm / 8 ft 6 in at the low end and 2845 mm / 9 ft 4 in at the high end.
- The 760 x 1220 mm / 30 x 48 in wheelchair space sits under the roof, parallel to the curb, overlapping the clear path.

## Structure

- **Posts:** two 3048 mm channels bolted back to back through their slotted backs, open faces toward the street and the back of walk.
- **Post base:** two 90° angle fittings per post, bolted into the post's open faces, flat legs pointing front and back, each anchored with two screw anchors. Anchor spread is about 229 mm / 9 in front to back, within the 305 mm post zone.
- **Arms:** a 1219 mm channel beside each post, open side toward the post, joined to the post's front and back faces with angle fittings. The two arms are set at different heights (2616 and 2819 mm / 8 ft 7 in and 9 ft 3 in to the arm top) to create the roof slope.
- **Knee braces:** one 610 mm channel per arm at 45°. End connections are pending engineering detail.
- **Beams:** two 3048 mm channels resting on the arms at the back and front edges, fixed with an angle fitting on each arm and a bolt and nut through the beam.
- **Purlins:** five 1219 mm channels on the beams at 0, 914, 1524, 2134 and 3048 mm (0, 3, 5, 7 and 10 ft).
- **Roof:** two corrugated polycarbonate panels laid lengthwise, screwed through the crests into the purlins, with closure strips at the ends.
- **Leaning rail:** one 3048 mm channel, slotted back against the posts' street faces, bolted straight into spring nuts. Top at 813 mm / 32 in.
- **Post extensions:** the posts rise above the roof and provide free mounting points for a stop flag, solar panel or real-time display.

## Materials and finish

- Retail electro-galvanized strut is accepted for cost and availability, with zinc-rich touch-up at every bolted joint and scratch, and a yearly inspection.
- Stainless or hot-dip galvanized fasteners and anchors are preferred where available.

## Bench add-on

- A second 3048 mm channel on the posts at seat height carries the bench, so the bench adds no anchors.
- Three 610 mm cross arms, two front legs that rise to become armrest posts, two armrests and two back uprights.
- Three seat boards and two back boards, 1320 mm / 52 in long, composite or preservative-treated. Seat height about 480 mm / 19 in.

## Documentation principles

- An IKEA-style manual that is mostly wordless, so it can be used across languages. Metric first, imperial second.
- Parts described generically by profile, size and finish, never by brand or store.
- A 1:1 hardware page with a print-scale check bar.
