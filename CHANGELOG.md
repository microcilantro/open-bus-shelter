# Changelog

## Rev A (draft for discussion), 2026-10-31

First public draft.

- Two-post cantilever shelter, 3048 mm / 10 ft, for sidewalks 1524 mm / 5 ft and wider.
- Stock-length strut only (10, 4 and 2 ft); no welding or metal cutting.
- Surface-mounted post base built from two angle fittings and four screw anchors per post.
- Leaning rail, plus an optional hanging bench for sidewalks about 7 ft and wider.
- 17-page wordless assembly manual, four drawing sheets, generic parts list and a US cost estimate.
- Open engineering items listed in `docs/engineering-review.md`.
